Week 8 SGTA Preview: Project Closure

Completion requirements

This week, we’ll explore what it takes to properly close a project. We’ll:

  • Keep working on your Sprint 1 - Week 2 work

  • Review key closure steps like lessons learned, documentation, and team wrap-up.

  • Examine the failed Denver Airport project to see what went wrong.

  • Work in groups to simulate closing a fictional project, including a report, reflection, and celebration.

  • Present our closure plans and debrief on why closure matters — even when projects don’t go to plan.
